# 从 Trial Landscape 到产业/金融研究

## Layer 1 — Clinical landscape
本项目完成：

- mechanism
- phase
- status
- sponsor
- endpoint
- eligibility
- geography
- completion timeline

## Layer 2 — Asset evidence
对重点资产补：

- conference readout
- publication
- effect size
- biomarker strategy
- adverse events
- discontinuation
- subgroup
- comparator

## Layer 3 — Regulatory
补：

- FDA / EMA / CDE
- breakthrough / accelerated pathways
- approval / CRL / advisory committee

## Layer 4 — Commercial
补：

- patient population
- diagnosis rate
- biomarker-testing requirement
- infusion/monitoring burden
- reimbursement
- launch sequencing
- competitor timing

## Layer 5 — Finance
补：

- market cap / EV
- cash & short-term investments
- quarterly burn
- runway
- debt
- dilution / ATM
- licensing terms
- catalyst calendar

## Asset Card
最终每个项目回答：

1. Science — mechanism 是否可信？
2. Clinical — trial design 和 readout 是否有临床意义？
3. Regulatory — 路径是否清晰？
4. Competitive — 赛道是否拥挤？
5. Commercial — 谁使用、谁支付？
6. Finance — 公司有没有钱等到关键读数？
7. Catalyst — 下一次价值重估是什么？
